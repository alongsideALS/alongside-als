On your phone

Use Alongside ALS as an app

You don't need an app store. Add this site to your home screen and it opens like an app, with its own icon, no browser bars, and the pages you've visited saved for when you have no signal.

iPhone or iPad

  1. Open this page in Safari (other browsers on iPhone can't add apps).
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen (the square with an arrow).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Look for the ribbon icon named "Alongside ALS" on your home screen.

Android phone or tablet

  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu in the top right, then Add to Home screen or Install app.
  3. Tap Install. Chrome may also offer an "Install" bar at the bottom of the page; that works too.

Computer

In Chrome or Edge, look for the small install icon at the right end of the address bar, or open the browser menu and choose Install Alongside ALS. It then opens in its own window from your dock, taskbar or Start menu.

What it does

What works offline, and what doesn't

Works without a connection: every page you have opened before, the grant, service and study lists once you have viewed them, the map and clinic directory once viewed, and your whole My plan list with notes and checklists. The site stores these on your device the first time you load them and refreshes them quietly whenever you're back online.

Needs a connection: links to the programs' own websites and ClinicalTrials.gov, the contact form, the ZIP code lookup the first time you use it, and the PDF trackers until they have been downloaded once.

Your privacy doesn't change. Nothing new is collected. Saved items stay in this browser on this device, exactly as on the website, and there is still no account. Removing the icon from your home screen removes the app; the saved list lives with the browser you installed it from.

Updates are automatic. When grants or studies are refreshed on the site, the app picks up the new lists the next time it opens with a connection. If a page tells you a new version is available, close and reopen the app.

Stuck? On iPhone the Add to Home Screen option only appears in Safari's Share menu, not in Chrome or Firefox. If the option is missing on Android, update Chrome and try again. Anything else, tell us which phone you have.
