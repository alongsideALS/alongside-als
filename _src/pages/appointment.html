Support beyond the appointment

  • Emotional support and connection →Peer communities and scheduled groups. Ask your care team about professional emotional support.
  • Caregivers, children and families →Practical support, respite, and family programs.
  • Benefits, work and future planning →Social Security, Medicare, VA and insurance help.
  • Find a care team or resource navigator →Help turning your questions into a manageable plan.

This checklist is a conversation aid, not medical advice. Do not wait for a routine appointment to seek urgent care.
