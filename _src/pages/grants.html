Grants

Money to help with what ALS costs

Grants and scholarships that pay for equipment, rides to clinic, respite care, prescriptions, home changes, children's needs and school. Each one shows when it's open, how much it gives and what to have ready. Pick your state to see local programs first.

Looking for Social Security, Medicare, VA benefits, equipment loans or free services? Those are under Services. Not sure what to have ready? See Get ready.

Before applying: funding is never guaranteed. Confirm availability, eligibility, amounts and deadlines directly with the program. "Up to" amounts are maximums, not promised payments.
Prefer to talk to a person? The ALS Association's information and referral line is 800-782-4747. Your local chapter's care coordinator can also tell you which programs fit your situation. For research questions, ALS TDI's Let Us Help team talks with families about studies.
