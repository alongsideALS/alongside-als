If the diagnosis is recent

Three things families say to do first

You don't have to do everything now. These three open the most doors, and each takes a phone call or a form.

  1. 1
    Register with your local ALS chapter

    They assign a care coordinator and unlock local grants and free equipment loans. Find your chapter or see Services.

  2. 2
    Apply for Social Security Disability and say "ALS"

    ALS is a Compassionate Allowance: no five-month wait, and Medicare follows. How to start.

  3. 3
    Start one folder of documents

    A signed diagnosis letter, insurance cards and receipts unlock almost every grant. The checklist.
