Questions about ALS benefits and support

Does ALS qualify for Social Security disability?

Yes. ALS is on Social Security's Compassionate Allowances list, so disability claims are fast-tracked, and the usual five-month waiting period for SSDI is waived for ALS. See Social Security Disability and Medicare.

When does Medicare start for someone with ALS?

For people with ALS, Medicare begins the same month SSDI benefits start, with no 24-month wait. It covers clinic visits, many tests and durable medical equipment. Free one-on-one help comparing plans is available from your state's SHIP Medicare counselors.

What VA support is there for veterans with ALS?

The VA treats ALS as presumptively service connected for veterans with 90 or more days of continuous active service, and it is typically rated at 100%. That opens VA disability compensation, health care, housing and automobile grants, Aid and Attendance and the family caregiver program. Paralyzed Veterans of America's service officers file VA claims for free.

Where can I borrow ALS equipment?

Many ALS Association chapters, ALS United organizations and state programs lend wheelchairs, lifts, communication devices and ramps at no cost. Filter the list above by "Equipment & loans" and your state, or check the map for programs near you.

Many of these are run by your local ALS Association chapter or ALS United organization. Registering with them is usually the first step. Find your chapter.
