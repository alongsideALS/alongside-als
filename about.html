About Alongside ALS

An independent guide to ALS resources, built to make a difficult search easier to navigate.

Who maintains this site?

Alongside ALS is an independent, volunteer-maintained guide. Its purpose is to help people living with ALS, caregivers, and families find practical assistance, understandable information, and research resources.

It is built and kept up by a family in Hamilton, Massachusetts, on the North Shore, going through ALS ourselves. We started it after spending too many evenings hunting through dozens of websites for the same few answers, and decided to put everything we found in one place for the next family. There is no company behind it, no funding, no advertising, and nothing for sale.

Alongside does not provide clinical care, award grants, or decide eligibility. It is independent of the organizations listed here, and no organization pays to be included.

How resources are selected

We prioritize official provider pages, public benefits agencies, established ALS organizations, and the official clinical-trial registry. Descriptions summarize what each source offers; inclusion is not a ranking or endorsement. Community discussions are labeled separately from medical evidence.

Grant entries distinguish open applications, closed rounds, waitlists, and information that needs confirmation. A published application does not guarantee funds. State filters help narrow a search but cannot decide whether you qualify.

Study information comes from a dated ClinicalTrials.gov snapshot. Short explanations describe the registry record and are not personalized matches. Studies marked "hand-checked" were also reviewed against recent announcements. Read the full record and ask the study team about eligibility and practical requirements.

Review dates reflect the underlying source check, not a cosmetic page edit. A working link does not confirm that a program is open.

Report outdated information or ask a question

Use the form below, or email alsalongside@gmail.com. If a listing looks out of date, tell us which page or organization, what appears incorrect, and an official source if you have one.

Please do not send medical records, financial documents, passwords, or application details. Messages go to a volunteer inbox that is not monitored for emergencies or clinical advice. Send applications directly to the provider.

Accessibility

The site supports keyboard navigation, large controls, a larger-text setting (the "AA" button in the header), and reduced-motion preferences. Please tell us if a page is difficult to use, including your device or assistive technology if you wish. Testing with screen readers, eye-gaze and switch access is an ongoing need; we do not claim certified accessibility compliance.

Privacy

Last updated October 17, 2026.

Browsing this guide

Alongside ALS does not ask you to create an account, fill out a contact form, upload documents, or submit a grant application. Its explorers run in your browser and load resource lists from this website. We have not added advertising trackers to the website code. To see how many people the site is helping, we use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts page views without cookies, without identifying individual visitors and without following you to other websites.

Saved items on your device

Choosing "Save", ticking a checklist item or adding a note stores that information in this browser's local storage, along with your filter choices. Alongside does not upload it or link it to an account. Anyone using this browser may see it. Use "Clear saved list" on the My plan page to remove saved items, or clear this site's browser data. Private browsing, storage restrictions, or clearing browser data can remove your list. Copy or download your plan if you need to keep a copy. Do not put private medical or financial information in search fields or notes you share.

Using the site as an app

You can add Alongside ALS to your phone's home screen (see Use as an app). When you do, your browser keeps a copy of the pages and lists you have opened so they work without a connection. That copy stays on your device, is managed by your browser, and sends nothing to us. Removing the icon removes the app; your saved plan stays with the browser it was made in.

Hosting and technical information

This website is hosted on Cloudflare Pages, which sets security cookies and processes technical information such as IP addresses, browser information, requested pages, and request times to operate the service, prevent abuse, and maintain security. See Cloudflare's privacy policy. Page fonts load from Google Fonts. No advertising service has been added by Alongside.

When you follow a link

Grant providers, forums, social networks, and other linked websites have their own privacy policies. They may collect personal information or use cookies when you visit or apply. Alongside does not receive your application documents through this guide.

Downloaded checklists and trackers

Information entered in a downloaded PDF is handled by your PDF application and any storage or sync service you choose. Alongside does not receive those entries. Keep completed files on a device you trust, and share them only through the intended provider's official process.

Disclaimer

Last updated October 17, 2026.

Medical information

Content is for general education and is not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. It does not establish a clinician–patient relationship. Discuss symptoms, medicines, supplements, equipment, and care decisions with a qualified healthcare professional who knows your circumstances. Do not delay seeking care because of something you read here. This website is not an emergency service. In an emergency, contact your local emergency services.

Grants, benefits, and financial guidance

Listings and application guides are general information, not individualized financial, insurance, tax, or legal advice. Eligibility, funding, award amounts, deadlines, service areas, and program rules can change. A listing does not guarantee acceptance or payment. Maximum awards are not promised amounts, and historical examples are not current award limits. Confirm details directly with the provider, including whether costs need prior approval and whether payment goes to you or a vendor.

External websites and communities

Links are offered as starting points. Inclusion does not imply endorsement, affiliation, sponsorship, or a guarantee of an organization's services. Names and marks belong to their respective owners. Forum posts and personal stories reflect individual experiences and may be inaccurate or unsuitable for your circumstances. Treat treatment claims as questions for your care team.

Sources and accuracy

Study facts are drawn from a dated ClinicalTrials.gov snapshot; full descriptions and eligibility requirements should be read on the official registry. A research phase is not evidence that a treatment is effective. We aim to make this guide useful and accurate, but it may contain errors, omissions, or outdated information. Confirm important details with the original source before relying on them.
