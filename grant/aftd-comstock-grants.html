Comstock Grants (respite and quality of life) for ALS with FTD

The Association for Frontotemporal Degeneration (AFTD)

Rolling · apply anytimeRespite & caregivingQuality of lifeEquipment & technology

Two $500 reimbursement grants for families facing frontotemporal degeneration, including FTD with ALS (ALS alone does not qualify). The Comstock Respite Grant gives a full-time unpaid care partner up to $500 per family per year for in-home care, adult day services, short-term overnight care, counseling or therapy, or well-being classes such as yoga. The Comstock Quality of Life Grant gives the person with FTD up to $500 for a product or service that improves daily life: communication devices and apps, internet costs, transportation, companion care, co-pays or medications, home adaptations, exercise classes. AFTD also runs a Comstock Travel Grant, which from July 1, 2026 only covers travel to AFTD's Education Conference (next: May 13-14, 2027 in Raleigh, NC); its guidelines are being updated, so ask the HelpLine.

Amount
Up to $500 per grant (Respite: up to $500 per family per year)
When to apply
Any time. Only expenses incurred after approval are reimbursed; respite funds should be used within 6 months. Quality of Life: one application per AFTD fiscal year (July 1 to June 30).
Who qualifies
  • Documented FTD diagnosis — the application lists FTD/ALS as a subtype, so a person with ALS qualifies only if FTD has also been diagnosed
  • U.S. residents; for the respite grant the care partner and the person with FTD must live together
  • Respite grant: the applicant is a full-time, unpaid care partner
  • Respite grant not available if the person with FTD already gets respite through hospice, Medicaid-covered services, the VA or other public healthcare benefits
What you'll need
  • Online application (or the printable PDF from the Comstock Grants page)
  • Diagnostic records showing how the FTD diagnosis was made (physician evaluation, neuropsychological testing or imaging report; a letter from a current physician may be accepted)
  • A description of how you'll use the money
  • Receipts on request after approval (reimbursement is submitted through AFTD's online form)
How to apply
Apply online at theaftd.org/comstock-respite-application (care partners) or theaftd.org/comstock-quality-of-life-application (person with FTD), or download the printable PDF from the Comstock Grants page. After approval, pay for the service or item and submit reimbursement through AFTD's online form. Questions: ComstockGrants@theaftd.org or the AFTD HelpLine 1-866-507-7222.
Contact
