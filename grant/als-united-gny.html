Patient Grant Program

ALS United Greater New York

Open · due Jan 15Respite & caregivingPrescriptionsEquipment & technology

Reimburses respite or home health care, prescription copays, and durable medical equipment that insurance doesn't cover.

Amount
Varies with available funds
When to apply
Two cycles a year. Due July 15 for receipts dated Jan 16 to Jul 15, and due Jan 15 for receipts dated Jul 16 to Jan 15. Grants depend on available funds.
Next deadline
January 15, 2027
Who qualifies
  • Diagnosed with ALS
  • Primary residence in the Greater New York service area
  • Registered with ALS United Greater New York
  • One grant per family per calendar year
What you'll need
  • Request for Funds form
  • Respite Provider Log (for respite)
  • Copies of receipts
  • Vendor invoice with exact pricing (for direct-to-vendor payment)
How to apply
Talk with your Care Coordinator first, then submit all forms and receipts together.
Contact
(212) 720-3059
