CFMD Individual Assistance Grant

Colorado Fund for Muscular Dystrophy

Rolling · apply anytimeEquipment & technologyHome modificationMedical costsQuality of life

Pays up to $1,000 directly to a vendor for equipment, assistive technology, therapies or services, medical supplies, home modifications, or a recreation center membership that insurance doesn't fully cover, for anyone in the US with a neuromuscular disorder, ALS included. It does not pay off loans or reimburse things already bought.

Amount
Up to $1,000 per application, paid to the vendor
When to apply
Apply any time; applications are reviewed as they arrive and grants depend on available funds. Only applicants chosen for a grant are notified.
Who qualifies
  • Lives in the United States (not Colorado-specific despite the name)
  • Diagnosed with any form of muscular dystrophy or a neuromuscular disorder; ALS is listed
  • Item or service is not fully covered by insurance and hasn't been purchased yet
What you'll need
  • Letter from your doctor verifying the diagnosis
  • Proof of cost (web link, invoice, or quote) and the vendor's contact details
  • Information on other resources you've used (insurance, Medicaid, Medicare, other grants this year)
How to apply
Complete the online application (JotForm) linked from the Grant Opportunities page, or email info@coloradofmd.org to request a printable form to mail. Incomplete applications aren't considered.
Contact
info@coloradofmd.org
Checked
October 22, 2026 against the organization's own page. Funding is never guaranteed; confirm details with the program before applying.
