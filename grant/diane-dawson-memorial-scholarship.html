Diane Dawson Memorial Scholarship

Sacramento Region Community Foundation

Opens in cyclesEducation & scholarshipsFamily & children

A $1,000-$3,000 scholarship for graduating high school seniors in the Sacramento region or the Denver area whose parent or guardian has a terminal illness, or who lost a parent to a terminal disease during high school.

Amount
$1,000-$3,000 (renewable up to one year by reapplying)
When to apply
Annual cycle; most recent deadline was March 13, 2026, next expected to open around December 2026
Who qualifies
  • Graduating senior at a high school in El Dorado, Placer, Sacramento or Yolo counties (CA) or the Denver area (CO)
  • Parent or legal guardian has terminal cancer or another terminal illness, or applicant lost a parent to a terminal disease during high school
  • Cumulative GPA 2.5 or higher and demonstrated financial need
  • Physician letter or death certificate required
How to apply
Create an account at sacregcf.academicworks.com and submit the supplemental questions, two recommendation letters and a one-page essay.
Contact
info@sacregcf.org
Checked
October 26, 2026 against the organization's own page. Funding is never guaranteed; confirm details with the program before applying.
