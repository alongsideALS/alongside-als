Harry Gregg Foundation Grants

Harry Gregg Foundation

Closing soon · 30 days leftEquipment & technologyHome modificationRespite & caregivingMedical costsEducation & scholarshipsQuality of life

Grants of up to $1,200 for New Hampshire residents with a long-term disability, for specialty equipment, services and supplies, modifications to living areas, work sites or vehicles, respite for the person or their caregivers, non-reimbursed medical or therapy treatments (up to $500), computers (up to $500) and adaptive software, vehicle repairs (up to $500), camps and recreation, and continuing education. Does not pay medical copays or deductibles, rent or mortgage, heating fuel, lift chairs, hearing aids, eyeglasses, dental care, or costs more than six months old; projects over $4,800 need other funding sources too. Not ALS-specific.

Amount
Up to $1,200 (often less); $500 caps for medical or therapy costs and computers
When to apply
Apply online any time; trustees decide three times a year. Next deadline Nov 23, 2026 (Dec 8 meeting), then Mar 22, 2027 (Apr 13, 2027 meeting) and late July for the August meeting. One application per six-month period; lifetime limit of four grants (five if one is for recreation).
Next deadline
November 23, 2026
Who qualifies
  • New Hampshire resident with a long-term physical, developmental or emotional disability; not ALS-specific, but ALS meets the physical-disability definition (a lasting barrier to daily tasks such as walking and driving)
  • Household income within the foundation's limits (examples: single adult $2,987/month; two adults, one working, no children $4,703; one adult and one child $4,990; two working adults and one child $5,530; ask the Grant Administrator for other households)
  • Funds must benefit the person with the disability; applicants under 21 count their parents' income
What you'll need
  • Letter of diagnosis from a physician or other qualified medical or social-service entity (required to submit)
  • Household income from all adults and sources (verification may be requested)
  • Paid receipt, invoice or written estimate for the item or service (vehicle repairs need a written estimate)
  • Signed Terms of Award after approval
How to apply
Online only through the 'Click here to apply' link on harrygreggfoundation.org/apply/ (grantinterface.com; create an account; tutorial available). No paper applications; ask a case manager or clinic if you lack computer access. Payment usually arrives 10-14 days after the signed Terms of Award and documentation are received. Questions: 603-792-0137 or hgf@crotchedmountain.org.
