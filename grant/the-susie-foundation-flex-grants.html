Flex Grants

The Susie Foundation

Rolling · apply anytimeEquipment & technologyHome modificationRespite & caregivingMedical costs

Reimbursement for eligible ALS-related equipment, home modifications, care, and medical costs not covered by other assistance.

Amount
Up to $2,000 per quarter, $4,000 per year
When to apply
Awarded quarterly as funds allow. First-time applicants and severe hardship get priority. New England residents only; see the guidelines PDF for quarterly dates.
Who qualifies
  • New England resident (CT, ME, MA, NH, RI, VT)
  • Definitive ALS diagnosis
What you'll need
  • Complete application
  • Diagnosis-verification form signed by a physician, neurologist or social worker (first-time applicants)
  • Receipts or documentation for eligible expenses
How to apply
Apply on the Susie Foundation Flex Grant page.

See program page
