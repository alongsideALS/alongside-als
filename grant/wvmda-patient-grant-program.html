Movement Disorder Patient Grant Program

West Virginia Movement Disorder Alliance

Confirm with the organizationTravelMedical costsPrescriptionsEquipment & technologyHome modification

Money toward the costs of getting care and staying mobile for people with a movement disorder, ALS included: travel to appointments and procedures, medical and medication copays, physical therapy, power chairs, scooters and other mobility equipment, and home accessibility ramps. Can cover past expenses (reimbursement) or future ones. Each request is reviewed individually against available funds; no cap is published.

Amount
Not published (you state the amount requested)
When to apply
No deadline published; apply any time, but funding depends on what's available when you apply and isn't guaranteed.
Who qualifies
  • Patient or caregiver dealing with a movement disorder; the program page lists ALS alongside Parkinson's, essential tremor, dystonia, MSA, Huntington's, MS, and others
  • Lives in West Virginia, eastern Kentucky, or southern Ohio
  • The form asks for a sponsoring WVMDA member, household income range, and last year's out-of-pocket health costs
What you'll need
  • Patient Grant Request Form (PDF)
  • Supporting documentation such as quotes or estimates
  • Insurance company and member/group number
  • Receipts and invoices as expenses occur, if approved
How to apply
Download the Patient Grant Request Form from wvmda.org/grant-program, describe the item or service and how it relates to your condition, attach quotes, and email it to info@wvmda.org or mail it to 5432 Peaceful Ridge, Lavalette, WV 25535. If you're unsure your need fits, email first.
Contact
info@wvmda.org
Checked
October 22, 2026 against the organization's own page. Funding is never guaranteed; confirm details with the program before applying.
