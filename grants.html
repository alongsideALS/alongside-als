Grants

Money to help with what ALS costs

Grants and scholarships that pay for equipment, rides to clinic, respite care, prescriptions, home changes, children's needs and school. Each one shows when it's open, how much it gives and what to have ready. Pick your state to see local programs first.

Looking for Social Security, Medicare, VA benefits, equipment loans or free services? Those are under Services. Not sure what to have ready? See Get ready.

Before applying: funding is never guaranteed. Confirm availability, eligibility, amounts and deadlines directly with the program. "Up to" amounts are maximums, not promised payments.
  • Peter Frates ALS Foundation (Pete Frates Family Foundation) · Nationwide
    ALS Home Health Care Assistance

    Helps people with ALS pay out-of-pocket home health care costs, such as caregiver pay and training, so they can stay at home. The foundation was started by the family of Pete Frates, who helped launch the Ice Bucket Challenge. Varies. Program website

  • National Organization for Rare Disorders (NORD) · Nationwide
    ALS Travel & Lodging Assistance

    Financial help with travel and lodging costs related to ALS care (see the program's resource document for covered expenses). NORD's Caregiver Respite Program and Rare Disease Educational Support program (both open to ALS families) are listed separately. Varies; not published. Program website

  • Vehicle manufacturers (Toyota, Honda, Ford, GM, Stellantis, Nissan, Subaru, Hyundai, Mazda, VW, Audi, BMW) · Nationwide
    Automaker mobility rebates for adaptive equipment

    Most automakers reimburse part of the cost of adaptive equipment (hand controls, lifts, ramps, power seats) installed on a new vehicle. Typical amounts: Toyota: up to $1,000; Honda: up to $1,000; Ford / Lincoln: up to $1,000; Chrysler / Dodge / Jeep / Ram: up to $1,000; GM (Chevrolet, GMC, Buick, Cadillac): Varies; check program site; Nissan: up to $1,000; Subaru: up to $1,000; Hyundai: $1,000; Mazda: up to $1,000; Volkswagen: up to $1,000; Audi: up to $1,500; BMW / MINI: up to $2,500. Ask the dealer about the program before you buy. Usually $1,000 (up to $1,500 at Audi; up to $2,500 at BMW/MINI). Program website

  • National Organization for Rare Disorders (NORD) · Nationwide
    Caregiver Respite Program

    Money to hire a short-term respite caregiver (an RN, LPN, CNA, home health aide, or a non-professional caregiver) so an unpaid family caregiver can attend a conference or event or simply have an afternoon or evening away. ALS counts as a rare disease, so the unpaid full-time family caregiver of a person with ALS can apply. Not published. Program website

  • Colorado Fund for Muscular Dystrophy · Nationwide
    CFMD Individual Assistance Grant

    Pays up to $1,000 directly to a vendor for equipment, assistive technology, therapies or services, medical supplies, home modifications, or a recreation center membership that insurance doesn't fully cover, for anyone in the US with a neuromuscular disorder, ALS included. It does not pay off loans or reimburse things already bought. Up to $1,000 per application, paid to the vendor. Program website

  • The ALS Association · Nationwide
    Community of Angels Fund

    Grants to help families with the costs of caregiving and care needs: respite care, communication devices, home modifications, medical care and related expenses. Created in memory of Jane Calmes. Varies; not published. Program website

  • The Association for Frontotemporal Degeneration (AFTD) · Nationwide
    Comstock Grants (respite and quality of life) for ALS with FTD

    Two $500 reimbursement grants for families facing frontotemporal degeneration, including FTD with ALS (ALS alone does not qualify). The Comstock Respite Grant gives a full-time unpaid care partner up to $500 per family per year for in-home care, adult day services, short-term overnight care, counseling or therapy, or well-being classes such as yoga. The Comstock Quality of Life Grant gives the person with FTD up to $500 for a product or service that improves daily life: communication devices and apps, internet costs, transportation, companion care, co-pays or medications, home adaptations, exercise classes. AFTD also runs a Comstock Travel Grant, which from July 1, 2026 only covers travel to AFTD's Education Conference (next: May 13-14, 2027 in Raleigh, NC); its guidelines are being updated, so ask the HelpLine. Up to $500 per grant (Respite: up to $500 per family per year). Program website

  • Dream Foundation · Nationwide

    Grants an experience or quality-of-life wish for an adult with a terminal or life-limiting illness, such as travel within the U.S. to see family, for the person, one caregiver and children at home. Covers the dream (often economy airfare, one hotel room and an activity). Program website

  • Muscular Dystrophy Association (MDA) · Nationwide

  • ALS Ride For Life · Nationwide
    Emotional Support Services Grant

    Up to 15 free, confidential counseling sessions with a licensed clinical social worker that ALS Ride For Life partners with and pays directly, for the person with ALS and for family members or significant others. Sessions can be by phone, Zoom or in person. Up to 15 counseling sessions, paid directly to the clinician. Program website

  • ALS Ride For Life · Nationwide
    End-of-Life Doula Support Grant

Sessions can be by phone, text, Zoom or in person, and you can start with a preliminary consultation at any stage of the illness. Up to 15 sessions, paid directly to the doula. Program website

  • ALS Recovery Fund · FL
    Patient Care Fund

    Speech-generating devices (after a speech evaluation at the Kessenich Family MDA ALS Center), bathing and toileting equipment Medicare often won't cover, and respite care. Up to $10,000 per year. Program website

  • ALS in the Heartland · NE, IA
    Patient Financial Request Program

    One-time help of up to $500 toward equipment, home modifications or other essential ALS-related expenses that support safety, independence and quality of life at home. Families are asked to check the chapter's Equipment Loan Program first for items it can lend instead. Up to $500, one time. Program website

  • ALS United Greater New York · NY, NJ, CT
    Patient Grant Program

    Reimburses respite or home health care, prescription copays, and durable medical equipment that insurance doesn't cover. Varies with available funds. Program website

    Quality of Life Grant

    Communication devices, medical expenses, home modifications, home care and transportation. The Northern Ohio care team calls the same program the ALS Care Grant; the last published application (October 2023) set a cap of $2,000 per person per year, funded by the Ohio legislature, for Ohio residents registered with the Association in designated counties. Varies. Program website

  • The ALS Association · VA
    Quality of Life Grant (Virginia)

    Communication devices, medical expenses, home modifications, home care and transportation. Varies. Program website

  • ALS United Ohio (Central & Southern Ohio / Northern Kentucky) · OH, KY
    Quality of Life Reimbursement Grant

    Reimburses disease-related expenses such as equipment, care, services, respite care, counseling, healthcare co-pays and home modifications. Some items require a prescription. Up to $1,200 per patient per year. Program website

  • ALS United Connecticut · CT
    Reimbursement Grants (Care Assist Grants)

    Reimbursement fund programs that help offset some of the financial burdens of living with ALS. Covered expenses and amounts are not published. The chapter's annual report calls these Care Assist Grants. It also lists a Specialty Equipment Reimbursement Program: up to $2,000 a year toward medical equipment its loan closet can't supply, such as folding power wheelchairs and custom power-wheelchair accessories. Varies; contact the organization. Program website

  • The ALS Association, Florida · FL
    Respite Care for ALS Caregivers

    Pays a substitute caregiver (an agency or someone you choose who isn't family) so the full-time family caregiver can take a break. $2,000, used within 6 months. Program website

  • ALS United Orange County · CA
    Respite Care Grant

    $500 grants toward respite care so the caregiver of a person with ALS in Orange County, California can take a break. The money goes straight to the care provider (the provider bills ALS United Orange County), and a family can receive one grant per quarter. $500 per grant, up to one grant per quarter. Program website

  • ALS United Illinois (formerly Greater Chicago) · IL
    Respite Care Program

    Makes it possible for families to receive intermittent care for the person with ALS from a third-party caregiver so family caregivers can rest. Not published; contact the organization. Program website

  • ALS in the Heartland · NE, IA
    Respite Grant Program

    Pays or reimburses the caregiver you choose (home health agency, hospice aide, or an individual) for short-term care of the person with ALS so the primary caregiver can take a break, up to a monthly cap the page doesn't state. Care is only covered from the application date and intake session onward, and funding ends when the person with ALS dies. Not published (monthly cap set by the program). Program website

  • The ALS Association, Alabama · AL
    Respite Program

    Paid respite hours through a respite agency so family caregivers get a break. Up to 80 hours per fiscal year. Program website

  • ALS United Rhode Island · RI
    Respite Program (annual stipend)

    An annual stipend for respite care through partner home health providers, which may include homemakers, certified nursing assistants and house cleaning, so family caregivers can take a break. Annual stipend; amount not published. Contact the organization.. Program website

  • The ALS Association · MO
    Respite Program (Missouri)

    Short-term respite care grants for family caregivers. $1,000 to $3,000. Program website

  • The ALS Association · IL
    Respite Program (Southern Illinois)

    Short-term respite care for family caregivers, helping keep the person with ALS at home. $1,000 to $3,000. Program website

  • The ALS Association, Florida · FL
    Rick and Sherry Murray Medical Futures Scholarship

    For relatives of someone with ALS who are studying medicine, a health field or social work. Varies. Program website

  • The ALS Association, Minnesota · MN
    Senator Tomassoni Caregiver Support Program

    Money toward in-home care, through a home care agency or a person you choose (not a spouse, parent or child), plus free ALS caregiver training. Varies. Program website

  • Les Turner ALS Foundation · IL
    Support Services, Respite, Assistive Tech & Transportation Grants

    Four grant programs: equipment and home modifications (Walter Boughton), caregiver respite (Dan Nelson), assistive technology, and rides to clinic (Stuart Rosen Fund). Also free equipment loans. Varies. Program website

  • ALS of Nevada · NV
    The Porrello Project

    Tickets and experiences for people with ALS in Nevada, such as weekday Las Vegas shows, AEG Presents events and helicopter rides over the Strip, paid for with 'Porrello Project Experience Dollars'. Started in memory of Joey Porrello to help people with ALS keep enjoying the world. Up to $500 in 'Experience Dollars' per family per 6-month period. Program website

  • ALS of Michigan · MI
    Thomas E. Worford, Jr. Transportation Program

    Reimburses up to $300 per calendar year of rides from independent transportation companies for people with ALS who can no longer drive or need wheelchair-accessible transport — for non-emergency medical appointments, family gatherings, community events, vacations and other outings. ALS of Michigan can also point you to providers with discounted rates. Up to $300 per calendar year. Program website

  • Goode Foundation × Emory ALS Center · GA
    Travel Assistance Program (overnight hotel stay)

    A free accessible hotel night at the Emory Conference Center Hotel before an Emory ALS Clinic appointment. Covered overnight stay. Program website

  • ALS of Utica · NY
    Wish Grant Program

    Money for 'anything that will improve the quality of your life or manage ALS easier', for registered ALS of Utica patients in Oneida and Herkimer counties, NY, who have been debilitated by the disease. You name the dollar amount you need; no maximum is published. Not published (you request an amount). Program website

  • ALS New Mexico · NM
    Zia ALwayS Cares Quality of Life Grant

    Reimburses up to $2,500 per family per fiscal year for ALS-related costs: respite care (home care agencies, private paid caregivers, or family caregivers who don't live at the patient's address), communication tablets and apps, prescription copays, respiratory, feeding-tube and nutrition costs, durable medical equipment, counseling and massage therapy, home accessibility changes (grab bars, bathroom and door work, ramps, lifts, a generator for ventilator users), vehicle modifications, and rides or one night's lodging for ALS clinic visits (gas receipts are not accepted). Other ALS-related costs may be considered with a one-page explanation. ALS New Mexico also runs a Pitre Family Home Modification Grant (one-time, up to $5,000 for ramps, bathroom work and widened doorways) and an AT&T Technology Grant (a laptop plus up to $1,500 of communication software, with training); each has its own short online application on the same Care Services page. Up to $2,500 per family per fiscal year (Feb 1 to Jan 31). Program website
