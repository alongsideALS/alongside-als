Grants

Money to help with what ALS costs

Grants and scholarships that pay for equipment, rides to clinic, respite care, prescriptions, home changes, children's needs and school. Each one shows when it's open, how much it gives and what to have ready. Pick your state to see local programs first.

Looking for Social Security, Medicare, VA benefits, equipment loans or free services? Those are under Services. Not sure what to have ready? See Get ready.

Before applying: funding is never guaranteed. Confirm availability, eligibility, amounts and deadlines directly with the program. "Up to" amounts are maximums, not promised payments.
  • Peter Frates ALS Foundation (Pete Frates Family Foundation) · Nationwide
    ALS Home Health Care Assistance

    Helps people with ALS pay out-of-pocket home health care costs, such as caregiver pay and training, so they can stay at home. The foundation was started by the family of Pete Frates, who helped launch the Ice Bucket Challenge. Varies (paid by direct deposit or mailed check).

  • National Organization for Rare Disorders (NORD) · Nationwide
    ALS Travel & Lodging Assistance

    Financial help with travel and lodging costs related to ALS care (see the program's resource document for covered expenses). Varies; not published.

  • Vehicle manufacturers (Toyota, Honda, Ford, GM, Stellantis, Nissan, Subaru, Hyundai, Mazda, VW, Audi, BMW) · Nationwide
    Automaker mobility rebates for adaptive equipment

    Most automakers reimburse part of the cost of adaptive equipment (hand controls, lifts, ramps, power seats) installed on a new vehicle. Typical amounts: Toyota: up to $1,000; Honda: up to $1,000; Ford / Lincoln: up to $1,000; Chrysler / Dodge / Jeep / Ram: up to $1,000; GM (Chevrolet, GMC, Buick, Cadillac): Varies; check program site; Nissan: up to $1,000; Subaru: up to $1,000; Hyundai: $1,000; Mazda: up to $1,000; Volkswagen: up to $1,000; Audi: up to $1,500; BMW / MINI: up to $2,500. Ask the dealer about the program before you buy. Usually $1,000 (up to $1,500 at Audi; up to $2,500 at BMW/MINI).

  • Patient Advocate Foundation · Nationwide
    Co-Pay Relief: ALS Fund

    Help with prescription drug copays, coinsurance and deductibles for insured patients. Varies by fund cycle.

  • The ALS Association · Nationwide
    Community of Angels Fund

    Grants to help families with the costs of caregiving and care needs: respite care, communication devices, home modifications, medical care and related expenses. Created in memory of Jane Calmes. Varies; not published.

  • Dream Foundation · Nationwide
    Dreams for Adults with Life-Limiting Illness

    Grants an experience or quality-of-life wish for an adult with a terminal or life-limiting illness, such as travel within the U.S. to see family, for the person, one caregiver and children at home. Covers the dream (often economy airfare, one hotel room and an activity).

  • Muscular Dystrophy Association (MDA) · Nationwide
    Durable Medical Equipment Grant Program

    Help getting durable medical equipment such as wheelchairs, lifts and canes. MDA members may also get discount codes for lift chairs and stair lifts, and Permobil attendant-control grants. Up to $1,000 per person per calendar year.

  • Mary Beth Benison Foundation · Nationwide
    Family assistance

    Financial support, equipment, transportation, and home accessibility needs. Amount per family not published (foundation gave over $65,000 in grants in 2025).

  • Paint for a Cure · Nationwide
    Family assistance

    Programs aimed at easing the financial burden of ALS for families. Varies (past awards of $5,000 and $10,000).

  • HARK ALS · Nationwide
    Family assistance

    Grants for equipment gaps, travel, household needs, and small home modifications. Amount not published.

  • Catfish Foundation · Nationwide
    Financial Assistance Grants

    Financial assistance and grants for people living with ALS. Varies.

  • Project Main Street · Nationwide
    Financial Help Fund

    Financial help for people with ALS who are facing hardship. Varies.

  • ALS Ride For Life · Nationwide
    Fred Bargetzi Caregiver Grant

    A one-time grant for respite care, paid directly to the chosen care provider. One-time respite grant (amount not published).

  • HealthWell Foundation · Nationwide
    HealthWell ALS Fund

    Prescription copays for ALS drugs (riluzole, Radicava, Qalsody, Nuedexta and others) and Medicare Part B premiums. Up to $15,000 per 12-month grant.

  • The ALS Association · Nationwide
    Hoffman ALS Financial Assistance Program

    Direct costs of getting to ALS multidisciplinary clinic visits: transportation, hotels, lost wages, and copays or deductibles. Up to $1,000 per year.

  • Live Like Lou Foundation · Nationwide
    Holiday Respite Grant

    A Visa gift card during the holiday season to give ALS families some breathing room. Visa gift card (amount varies).

  • Matt's Place Foundation · Nationwide
    Home Accessibility Support

    Help with accessible home modifications and renovations for ALS families. Varies.

  • U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs · Nationwide
    Home Improvements and Structural Alterations (HISA) Grant

    Lifetime benefit for medically necessary home modifications to a veteran's primary residence: entry/exit access, roll-in showers, sink and counter access, permanent ramps, and plumbing or electrical work for home medical equipment. Does not cover portable ramps, stair glides, porch lifts or routine maintenance. Up to $6,800 lifetime for service-connected disabilities; up to $2,000 for non-service-connected.

  • Hope Loves Company (with Paint for A Cure) · Nationwide
    Hope Grants

    Direct financial support for young families affected by ALS (children under 18 at home) for expenses insurance doesn't cover: durable medical equipment, home care, ALS-related travel, home or auto modifications, communication and smart-home technology, generators. Paid as reimbursement or prepaid expense. Requests up to $6,000 (applications currently closed).

  • Bryon Riesch Paralysis Foundation · Nationwide
    Individual grants

    Charitable grants for people with paralysis to support independence. The currently linked application, dated 2022, states grants typically range from $2,000-$7,500, with no minimum.

  • axeALS Foundation · Nationwide
    Individual Grants for People with ALS

    Grants that help with the costs of living with ALS for people in significant financial need. Varies.

  • Live Like Lou Foundation · Nationwide
    Iron Horse Scholarship

    College or other post-secondary education for dependents of a parent or guardian with ALS. Renewable for up to four years, and finalists may receive a one-time Onward Award. Up to $2,130 per semester, up to 8 semesters (paid to the school).

  • The ALS Association · Nationwide
    Jane Calmes ALS Scholarship Fund

    Help toward an accredited college degree or vocational certificate for students whose ability to pay is severely limited by their family's ALS costs. Up to $2,500 per semester or $5,000 per year.

  • The ALS Association, local chapters · Nationwide
    Local Chapter Care Grants (find yours)

    Most local chapters run quality-of-life or care-services grants for respite, equipment, communication devices and home modifications. Amounts and rules vary by chapter. Varies by chapter.

  • Mary Jane Sanders ALS Scholarship · Nationwide
    Mary Jane Sanders ALS Scholarship

    A scholarship for high school seniors and undergraduates who have a parent with ALS or who lost a parent to ALS. $1,000.

  • Ventura County Community Foundation · Nationwide
    Merfeld Family Foundation Scholarship

    College scholarships for students whose parent has been diagnosed with ALS. Preference goes to Southern California and Iowa. $2,500.

  • Modest Needs Foundation · Nationwide
    Modest Needs Emergency Grants

    Small, one-time emergency grants (paid directly to the creditor or vendor) for low-income working households facing an unexpected expense that could push them into poverty. Not ALS-specific. Varies; small one-time grants.

  • Cocktails & Caregivers · Nationwide
    Our Tribe childcare & caregiver grants

    Caregiver grants, community, and help with medical travel or family needs. One-time grant, roughly a month of childcare (a $2,000 award was reported in 2023).

  • Racing for ALS · Nationwide
    Patient Assistance Fund

    ALS-related medical, care and home-modification costs. Approved applicants may also receive a free hands-free Giraffe drinking bottle. Grant amount varies (started at $1,000).

  • Paula Kovarick Segalman Scholarship Program · Nationwide
    Paula Kovarick Segalman Scholarship

    Undergraduate scholarship for students whose finances were affected by an ALS diagnosis, their own or a parent's or guardian's. $5,000.

  • Live Like Lou Foundation · Nationwide
    Quality-of-Life Grant

    Home accessibility improvements, respite care, extracurricular activities for children, payments toward an accessible vehicle, and other day-to-day needs. Expanded in 2025 from the earlier Home Improvement Grant. Up to $2,500 per family.

  • U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs · Nationwide
    Specially Adapted Housing (SAH) & SHA Grants

    Building, buying or adapting a home for accessibility (ramps, wider doors, roll-in showers). Veterans with service-connected ALS generally qualify. The VA treats ALS as presumptively service connected after 90+ days of continuous active service. FY2026: SAH up to $126,526 and SHA up to $25,350 (adjusted yearly).

  • U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs · Nationwide
    VA Automobile Allowance and Adaptive Equipment Grant

    A one-time payment toward a specially equipped vehicle (paid directly to the seller), plus adaptive-equipment grants for items like power seats, lifts and hand controls. ALS is an explicitly qualifying condition. Up to $27,908.90 one-time (effective Oct 1, 2026) plus adaptive equipment grants.

  • ALS United Mid-Atlantic · PA, NJ, DE
    Accessibility Program (loaner ramps and stair glide rental)

    Funds installation of temporary loaner modular ramps and/or a rental stair glide. Payments go directly to vendors and installers. Families may be asked to cost-share on a case-by-case basis. Also offers remote in-home accessibility evaluations. Varies; vendor-paid, family cost share decided case by case.

  • ALS Arizona · AZ
    ALS Arizona Care Grant

    Help with equipment, caregiving and transportation costs for people with ALS in Arizona (the chapter page now describes a general Care Grant rather than a separate respite program). Not published; contact the organization.

  • ALS Northwest · OR, WA
    ALS Financial Assistance Program

    Reimburses eligible ALS expenses, including mileage to medical appointments and respite care. Up to $500 per period, $1,000 per fiscal year.

  • ALS United North Carolina · NC
    ALS United North Carolina Care Grant

    Reimburses ALS-related costs that insurance and other programs usually don't cover: respite care, travel to ALS clinics and NC clinical trials, accessible home and vehicle modifications, communication devices and environmental controls, and generators for breathing equipment. Up to $500 per period (2 periods per year).

  • The ALS Association (Wisconsin) · WI
    Brian Trinastic Memorial Grant Program

    Reimburses costs not covered by insurance related to communication, medical expenses, home modifications and transportation needs. Varies; not published.

  • The ALS Association, Tennessee · TN
    Bronczek Blessings Angel Fund

    Quality-of-life grants for home modifications, respite care, groceries, lawn care and other ALS costs. Varies.

  • The ALS Association, Florida · FL
    Care Assist, Daily Living & Transportation Grants

    Care Assist grants for respite, medical expenses, home and vehicle modifications and van purchases. Daily Living grants for assistive technology. Transportation grants for rides to an ALS Certified Center or affiliated clinic. Varies (see Grant Guidelines).

  • ALS Network · CA
    Care for Kids Grant

    Small grants for tutoring, sports, lessons, school supplies and activities for children and young adults living with a loved one who has ALS. Small grants (amount varies).

  • The ALS Association, Indiana · IN
    Clevenger Family Financial Assistance Program

    Expenses that insurance usually doesn't cover, for families living with ALS or PLS. Up to $2,500 per year.

  • Compassionate Care ALS (CCALS) · MA, RI, NH, CT, VT, ME
    Equipment, Grants & Family Support

    Equipment lending, family grants and support programs, based on Cape Cod with wider New England reach. Varies.

  • Jeff Coombs Fund · MA
    Family Grants

    Assistance for unexpected family expenses related to illness, injury, loss, or other hardship. Not limited to ALS. Amount not published.

  • Fanny Pack Foundation · SC, NC, VA
    Family Support Grants

    Essential caregiving, medical equipment, ADA home renovations, and memory-making trips. Varies.

  • The Susie Foundation · CT, ME, MA, NH, RI, VT
    Flex Grants

    Reimbursement for eligible ALS-related equipment, home modifications, care, and medical costs not covered by other assistance. Up to $2,000 per quarter, $4,000 per year.

  • The ALS Association, Massachusetts · MA
    Foundation for Life Grant

    Paid directly to vendors for ALS costs insurance doesn't fully cover, such as wheelchairs, stair lifts, bathroom modifications, and speech or eye-gaze devices. Varies (paid to vendors).

  • ALS United Rocky Mountain · CO, UT, WY
    Goldhirsh Family Quality of Life Grant

    Reimburses respite, housekeeping, counseling, medical alert systems, AAC setup, rides to appointments, home modifications (ramps, grab bars, showers), clinic copays, massage and acupuncture, and AFO or splint copays. Varies with available funds.

  • The ALS Association · PA
    Grant Program (Western Pennsylvania)

    Home modifications, speech-generating devices, power wheelchairs, and respite or home care. Varies with available funds.

  • ALS United Rhode Island · RI
    Home Modification Program (one-time stipend)

    A one-time stipend toward home modifications that improve accessibility and safety, such as ramps or bathroom changes. Partner vendors also offer special pricing on modification services and equipment. One-time stipend; amount not published. Contact the organization..

  • ALS United Mid-Atlantic · PA, NJ, DE
    Howard I. Abrams In-Home Care Grant Program

    Helps pay some of the cost of in-home personal care for people with ALS who cannot afford adequate care on their own. Varies; not published.

  • ALS United of Georgia · GA
    Kostiuk ALS Care Grant

    Reimburses out-of-pocket ALS expenses for equipment, medical care and services insurance doesn't cover. Up to $1,000 per cycle, $2,000 per year.

  • ALS Network · CA
    L.L. Foundation for Youth Fund

    Respite care and medical equipment for the person with ALS, to lighten the load on young family caregivers. Varies.

  • The ALS Association · IA, MN, ND, SD, WI
    Maurer-Heinsen Home Access Program

    Pays a significant portion of the cost of a temporary modular ramp (vendor-installed) so people with ALS can get in and out of their homes. The family pays the agreed remainder; payment goes directly to the vendor. Ramps are retrieved and reused when no longer needed. Varies; the Association covers part of the cost and the family pays the rest (cost share agreed after vendor evaluation).

  • The ALS Association · IL, MO, KS, IN, NE
    Modular Ramp Program

    Funding and/or materials for a modular wheelchair ramp from the Association's ramp partner (AmRamp St. Louis). Ramp pieces are later donated to the Ramp Re-Use Program. 60% of ramp cost from AmRamp St. Louis, up to $4,800.

  • Rockin4ALS · WI
    Never Walk Alone Quality-of-Life Grant

    Unrestricted support for household costs, equipment, medical expenses, or meaningful personal experiences. $1,000 per grant.

  • Brigance Brigade Foundation · MD
    PALS Grant Program

    In-home care, equipment and home accessibility for people living with ALS or PLS. Up to $5,000 per calendar year.

  • ALS Recovery Fund · FL
    Patient Care Fund

    Speech-generating devices (after a speech evaluation at the Kessenich Family MDA ALS Center), bathing and toileting equipment Medicare often won't cover, and respite care. Up to $10,000 per year.

  • ALS United Greater New York · NY, NJ, CT
    Patient Grant Program

    Reimburses respite or home health care, prescription copays, and durable medical equipment that insurance doesn't cover. Varies with available funds.

  • ALS Ride for Life · NY
    Patient Grants & Advocacy

    Grant funding and advocacy services for people with ALS. Varies.

  • The ALS Association (Illinois areas) · IL
    Quality of Life Grant

    Respite care, communication devices, home modifications, medical care and other expenses. Varies.

  • The ALS Association · OH
    Quality of Life Grant (Ohio)

    Communication devices, medical expenses, home modifications, home care and transportation. Varies.

  • The ALS Association · VA
    Quality of Life Grant (Virginia)

    Communication devices, medical expenses, home modifications, home care and transportation. Varies.

  • ALS United Ohio (Central & Southern Ohio / Northern Kentucky) · OH, KY
    Quality of Life Reimbursement Grant

    Reimburses disease-related expenses such as equipment, care, services, respite care, counseling, healthcare co-pays and home modifications. Some items require a prescription. Up to $1,200 per patient per year.

  • ALS United Connecticut · CT
    Reimbursement Grants

    Reimbursement fund programs that help offset some of the financial burdens of living with ALS. Covered expenses and amounts are not published. Varies; contact the organization.

  • The ALS Association, Florida · FL
    Respite Care for ALS Caregivers

    Pays a substitute caregiver (an agency or someone you choose who isn't family) so the full-time family caregiver can take a break. $2,000, used within 6 months.

  • ALS United Illinois (formerly Greater Chicago) · IL
    Respite Care Program

    Makes it possible for families to receive intermittent care for the person with ALS from a third-party caregiver so family caregivers can rest. Not published; contact the organization.

  • The ALS Association, Alabama · AL
    Respite Program

    Paid respite hours through a respite agency so family caregivers get a break. Up to 80 hours per fiscal year.

  • ALS United Rhode Island · RI
    Respite Program (annual stipend)

    An annual stipend for respite care through partner home health providers, which may include homemakers, certified nursing assistants and house cleaning, so family caregivers can take a break. Annual stipend; amount not published. Contact the organization..

  • The ALS Association · MO
    Respite Program (Missouri)

    Short-term respite care grants for family caregivers. $1,000 to $3,000.

  • The ALS Association · IL
    Respite Program (Southern Illinois)

    Short-term respite care for family caregivers, helping keep the person with ALS at home. $1,000 to $3,000.

  • The ALS Association, Florida · FL
    Rick and Sherry Murray Medical Futures Scholarship

    For relatives of someone with ALS who are studying medicine, a health field or social work. Varies.

  • The ALS Association, Minnesota · MN
    Senator Tomassoni Caregiver Support Program

    Money toward in-home care, through a home care agency or a person you choose (not a spouse, parent or child), plus free ALS caregiver training. Varies.

  • Les Turner ALS Foundation · IL
    Support Services, Respite, Assistive Tech & Transportation Grants

    Four grant programs: equipment and home modifications (Walter Boughton), caregiver respite (Dan Nelson), assistive technology, and rides to clinic (Stuart Rosen Fund). Also free equipment loans. Varies.

  • ALS of Nevada · NV
    The Porrello Project

    A program founded by Joey Porrello to improve the experience of living with ALS and help people with ALS keep interacting with the world. Specific benefits are described in the application guidelines PDF on the program page. Not published; see application guidelines.

  • Goode Foundation × Emory ALS Center · GA
    Travel Assistance Program (overnight hotel stay)

    A free accessible hotel night at the Emory Conference Center Hotel before an Emory ALS Clinic appointment. Covered overnight stay.
