Help near you, on a map

Multidisciplinary ALS clinics, study sites that are looking for participants, and the grants and services that only serve certain states. Choose a state on the map, or enter your ZIP code to see what is within driving distance.

United States only for now. Clinics come from the ALS Association's certified-center list, the MDA ALS Care Center network, the VA and the NEALS research consortium; study sites come from the same ClinicalTrials.gov snapshot as the Trials page. Many clinics also offer telehealth visits — ask when you call.

Pins are placed by ZIP code or city, not exact street address. Study sites show studies that are recruiting, opening soon or enrolling by invitation.

Not sure which clinic to call? Your local ALS Association chapter can tell you which clinic is closest and help with the first appointment: 800-782-4747. Veterans can ask any VA medical center for its ALS coordinator. Several clinics also see people by video.
