Advance knowledge

Participate in research

Participation can include surveys, registries, natural-history studies, sample donation, or a clinical trial. Each has its own consent and eligibility rules.

  • Join the National ALS Registry ↗For diagnosed people in the United States; includes surveys and optional research notifications.
  • ALS Research Collaborative ↗Observational research with remote participation options for people with ALS and some asymptomatic gene carriers.
  • ALL ALS ↗NIH-funded natural-history studies; check current cohorts and participating sites.
  • ALS TDI Guided Trial Finder ↗A starting point for possible matches; public listings do not contain every eligibility detail.
  • National ALS Biorepository ↗Registry enrollment and informed consent are required; postmortem donation needs advance planning.

Research participation is voluntary. For interventional studies, discuss possible risks, benefits, travel, costs, and standard-care alternatives with the study team and your ALS clinician.
