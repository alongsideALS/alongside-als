Air Charity Network (Angel Flight / Mercy Flight member groups)

Air Charity Network

Available nowCaregiving & education

Free flights by volunteer pilots to distant medical facilities, specialists and clinical trials for people who cannot afford or physically manage commercial travel. Regional members include Angel Flight Northeast, Angel Flight Central, Angel Flight (OK/TX/AR/MO/KS) and Mercy Flight Southeast.

Amount
Free
When to apply
Ongoing; non-emergency, time-flexible trips only. Service not guaranteed.
Who qualifies
  • Financially distressed or otherwise unable to travel by public transportation
  • Medically stable enough to fly in a small unpressurized aircraft; see Passenger Qualifications
What you'll need
  • Appointment information
  • Physician release may be required by the regional member
How to apply
Use the 'Request a Flight' page to find the member organization for your region and submit a request.
Contact
877-621-7177, info@MercyMail.org
Checked
October 24, 2026 against the organization's own page. Funding is never guaranteed; confirm details with the program before applying.
