Paul B. Williams ALS Transportation Program

ALS United of Georgia

Available nowTravel

Arranges and pays for door-to-door non-emergency rides (wheelchair, stretcher, or ambulatory) to ALS clinic and doctor appointments or social outings, and short accessible-van rentals through partner companies, for Georgians living with ALS. Also offers financial help toward adapting your own van or car (pre-approval required) and a $1,000 van voucher toward buying an accessible van (written request; chosen on need and impact).

Amount
Free rides and van rentals; $1,000 van voucher and adaptation help by need
When to apply
Apply any time; submit each request at least two weeks before the appointment or rental. One application per trip or rental.
Who qualifies
  • Person with ALS living in Georgia
  • A caregiver or companion must ride along and stay with the person on every non-emergency transport
  • Each trip needs pre-approval and an authorization code from the program coordinator; cancel 48 hours ahead to avoid a fee
  • Van voucher and adaptation grants are awarded on need and impact
What you'll need
  • Transportation Program Application (online or PDF): request type, appointment date and time, pick-up and drop-off addresses, mobility equipment used
  • Name and phone of the adult accompanying you; signatures from both of you
  • For van adaptation or voucher requests, a written statement with vehicle and cost details
How to apply
Complete the online Transportation Program Application at alsgeorgia.org/navigating-als/transportation-program-application/ (or download the PDF) at least two weeks ahead, or call care services at 404-636-9909 to get started.
Contact
404-636-9909 · info@alsgeorgia.org
