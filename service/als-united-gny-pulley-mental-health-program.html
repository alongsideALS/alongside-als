Tom & Jill Pulley Mental Health and Wellness Program

ALS United Greater New York

Confirm with the organizationCaregiving & educationFamily & kidsMedical costs

Free psychotherapy with the chapter's licensed clinical social worker for people with ALS and family members (processing the diagnosis, physical changes, caregiver stress, planning ahead), plus Patient and Family Grants for Mental Health: supplemental grants that help pay for a specialized outside therapist, such as one who works with children coping with a parent's diagnosis, speaks your language, or specializes in a particular age group or stage of ALS.

Amount
Free therapy; grant amounts not published
When to apply
Ongoing; ask your care coordinator. Grant caps and any intake dates aren't published.
Who qualifies
  • People with ALS and their family members in ALS United Greater New York's area: New York City, Long Island, the Lower and Mid-Hudson Valley, and northern and central New Jersey
  • The chapter's other grants require registration with the chapter; confirm with your coordinator
How to apply
Contact your ALS United Greater New York care coordinator (regional coordinators are listed on the Contact Us page) or call 212-619-1400 and ask about the Pulley Mental Health and Wellness Program.
Contact
212-619-1400 · als@als-ny.org
