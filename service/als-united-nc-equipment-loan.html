Equipment Loan Program

ALS United North Carolina

Available nowEquipment & loans

Loans of seven types of durable medical equipment (power, manual, transport and travel power wheelchairs, rolling shower wheelchairs, rollators, electric Hoyer lifts) from four loan closets run with Stalls Medical and Adaptive Vans, with free delivery and pickup, for North Carolina residents with ALS who are uninsured, cannot order their own chair, or are awaiting a custom chair.

Amount
Free
When to apply
Ongoing; subject to inventory
Who qualifies
  • North Carolina resident
  • Verified ALS diagnosis
  • Equipment must stay in NC except for car/van travel
How to apply
Contact Diana Van Valen by email (on page) or at 919-390-0122.
