Biogen Support Services for QALSODY (tofersen)

Biogen

Available nowPrescriptions helpInsurance help

Support coordinators, insurance help, and two separate copay programs: one for the drug and one for administration costs (anesthesia, imaging, surgical procedure or drug administration) for SOD1-ALS patients prescribed QALSODY.

Amount
Copay assistance; amount not published
When to apply
Ongoing.
Who qualifies
  • U.S. resident prescribed QALSODY with non-government insurance (regardless of income)
  • Medicare, Medicaid, VA/DoD, TRICARE and other government coverage may not be eligible
  • Administration copay program excludes residents of MA, MN and RI
What you'll need
  • Prescription
  • Insurance information
  • Copay Reimbursement Form submitted by your provider for administration costs
How to apply
Call Biogen Support Services at 877-725-7639; enroll in each copay program separately.
Contact
