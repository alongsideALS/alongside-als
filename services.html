Services & benefits

Benefits, loans and free help

Government benefits like Social Security Disability, Medicare and VA compensation, plus free services: equipment and ramp loans, communication help, insurance case management, caregiver training, family programs and experiences.

Looking for money you apply for? See Grants.

  • Air Charity Network · Nationwide
    Air Charity Network (Angel Flight / Mercy Flight member groups)

    Free flights by volunteer pilots to distant medical facilities, specialists and clinical trials for people who cannot afford or physically manage commercial travel. Regional members include Angel Flight Northeast, Angel Flight Central, Angel Flight (OK/TX/AR/MO/KS) and Mercy Flight Southeast. Free.

  • ALS Hope Foundation · Nationwide
    ALS Hope Foundation

    Clinical care, support, education, and research programs for the ALS community. Free service.

  • The Assistance Fund (TAF) · Nationwide
    Amyotrophic Lateral Sclerosis (ALS) Fund

    Copay, coinsurance, deductible, health insurance premium and incidental medical expense assistance for FDA-approved ALS treatments. Note: the program has been listed as on a waitlist; confirm current status before applying. Varies; assistance through end of calendar year, renew annually.

  • ARCH National Respite Network · Nationwide
    ARCH National Respite Locator and State Lifespan Respite Programs

    Searchable locator for respite providers in your area, plus links to state Lifespan Respite programs, some of which offer respite vouchers to family caregivers. Varies; some states offer respite vouchers.

  • Biogen · Nationwide
    Biogen Support Services for QALSODY (tofersen)

    Support coordinators, insurance help, and two separate copay programs: one for the drug and one for administration costs (anesthesia, imaging, surgical procedure or drug administration) for SOD1-ALS patients prescribed QALSODY. Copay assistance; amount not published.

  • The ALS Association · Nationwide
    Caregiver Education Certification Course

    Online course on the physical and emotional effects of ALS, safe care, helpful and harmful interventions, and care planning. Free.

  • Bridging Voice · Nationwide
    Communication Device & Voice Banking Help

    Free help with voice banking, choosing a speech-generating device, and securing insurance or grant funding for it. Free services.

  • Live Like Lou Foundation · Nationwide
    Connect & Serve (volunteer help at home)

    Local volunteers help with one-time or short-term projects such as yard work, seasonal cleanup, painting, outdoor repairs and holiday lights. Free volunteer help.

  • Do It For The Love · Nationwide
    Do It For The Love

    Live music wish experiences for eligible applicants. This is an experience program rather than unrestricted cash assistance. Free service.

  • Healthcare Hospitality Network · Nationwide
    Healthcare Hospitality Network (hospitality house directory)

    Directory of hospitality houses near medical centers that offer lodging to patients and caregivers at little or no cost while traveling for treatment. Free or low cost (set by each house).

  • Hope-JG Foundation · Nationwide
    Hope-JG Foundation

    A foundation lead retained from the 2025 grant list. Amount unverified; a current individual grant program has not been confirmed.

  • The ALS Association · Nationwide
    Independence & Mobility Program (equipment loans)

    Education on mobility, independence and communication options, plus a free loan inventory of equipment and devices. Free loans.

  • The ALS Association with Patient Advocate Foundation · Nationwide
    Insurance & Benefits Resource Line

    Free one-on-one case management for insurance denials, Medicare and Medicaid questions, disability applications and other benefits. Free.

  • Mitsubishi Tanabe Pharma America · Nationwide
    JourneyMate Support Program (Radicava / Radicava ORS)

    Out-of-Pocket Savings Program (commercially insured patients pay as little as $0 per prescription up to an annual maximum), a free-drug Patient Assistance Program for uninsured patients with income up to 5x the Federal Poverty Level, and Insurance & Access Specialists who help with coverage questions. Veterans can get Radicava ORS at no cost through the VA. As little as $0 copay (commercial); free drug for eligible uninsured.

  • Les Turner · Nationwide
    Les Turner: ALS & Money Guide

    An introduction to financial planning, insurance, benefits, and possible assistance. Free service.

  • Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services · Nationwide
    Medicare (starts with SSDI for ALS)

    For people with ALS, Medicare coverage begins the same month SSDI benefits start, with no 24-month wait. It covers clinic visits, many tests, durable medical equipment like wheelchairs and BiPAP, and hospice. Health coverage.

  • NeedyMeds · Nationwide
    Medication Assistance Directory & Discount Card

    A searchable directory of manufacturer patient-assistance programs and copay funds for ALS drugs, plus a free drug discount card. Varies by program.

  • Mercy Medical Angels · Nationwide
    Mercy Medical Angels

    Free transportation to medical care: volunteer pilot flights (Mid-Atlantic), commercial airfare, ground transportation and gas cards nationwide. Free.

  • Administration for Community Living / Area Agencies on Aging · Nationwide
    National Family Caregiver Support Program (NFCSP)

    Through local Area Agencies on Aging: caregiver information and referral, counseling, support groups, training, respite care (in-home, adult day or facility) and limited supplemental services. Free or low cost; respite amounts vary by area.

  • Otsuka / Avanir · Nationwide
    Nuedexta Co-pay Savings Card and Otsuka Patient Assistance Foundation

    Copay savings card for commercially insured patients taking Nuedexta (for pseudobulbar affect), and a free-drug patient assistance program through the Otsuka Patient Assistance Foundation for uninsured or underinsured patients. As little as $0 per 90-day or $20 per 30-day prescription (commercial); free drug for eligible uninsured/underinsured.

  • U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs · Nationwide
    Program of Comprehensive Assistance for Family Caregivers (PCAFC)

    For a primary family caregiver of an eligible veteran: a monthly stipend, CHAMPVA health insurance if uninsured, mental health counseling, travel benefits, and at least 30 days of respite care per year for the veteran. Secondary caregivers get counseling and travel benefits. Monthly stipend (varies by location and level of care).

  • Rebuilding Together · Nationwide
    Rebuilding Together (local affiliates)

    Local affiliates and volunteers provide home repairs and accessibility modifications for low-income homeowners, including people with disabilities. Services, eligibility and waitlists vary by affiliate. Typically no cost to the homeowner (varies by affiliate).

  • Social Security Administration · Nationwide
    Social Security Disability (SSDI) & Medicare

    Monthly disability income. ALS is a Compassionate Allowance, so claims are fast-tracked. The 5-month SSDI waiting period is waived for ALS, and Medicare starts when SSDI begins. Based on your work history.

  • AT3 Center / Administration for Community Living · Nationwide
    State Assistive Technology (AT Act) Programs

    Every state and territory runs a federally funded AT program offering device loans (try before you buy), demonstrations, reused/refurbished equipment, and in many states low-interest financial loans to buy assistive technology, vehicles modifications or home modifications. Free device loans and demos; financial loan terms vary by state.

  • Hope Loves Company · Nationwide
    Support for Children & Young Adults (camps, scholarships)

    Free camps, programs and scholarships for children and young adults who have or had a loved one with ALS. Varies (scholarships and free camp programs).

  • Live Like Lou Foundation · Nationwide
    Talk to Me, Goose! communication app

    Free access to a text-to-speech app with personal voice cloning, so you can keep communicating in your own voice as speech changes. Free.

  • Team Hilliard Foundation · Nationwide
    Team Hilliard Foundation

    ALS awareness, research, treatment, and family-support mission. Free service.

  • Team Gleason · Nationwide
    Technology, Equipment & Care Services

    Communication devices, eye-gaze and smart-home technology, power-chair accessories and other equipment not covered by insurance. Also care services and 'Adventure' experiences. Equipment or service provided directly.

  • The PHAALS Foundation · Nationwide
    The PHAALS Foundation

    Family support through its Hall of Fame, Diamond Dreams, and LEGEND scholarship programs. No current dollar range verified for family support or LEGEND scholarships.

  • U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs · Nationwide
    VA Aid and Attendance

    A monthly payment added to a VA pension for veterans and survivors who need help with daily activities such as bathing, feeding or dressing, are bedridden, or live in a nursing home. Monthly addition to VA pension; rate varies.

  • U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs · Nationwide
    VA Disability Compensation for ALS

    Tax-free monthly compensation, health care, auto and housing grants, and caregiver support. ALS is presumptively service connected and typically rated at 100%. Based on rating and dependents.

  • The ALS Association · Nationwide
    Virtual Home Assessment

    A virtual walk-through of your home that recommends safety changes and modifications. Free.

  • Well Spouse Association · Nationwide
    Well Spouse Association

    Peer support for spouses and partners caring for someone with a chronic illness or disability: 25+ monthly support groups (including phone groups), 24/7 online support, respite events, webinars and an annual conference. Membership $39 per year.

  • Wish of a Lifetime · Nationwide
    Wish of a Lifetime from AARP

    Grants wishes (personalized experiences such as reunions and trips) to older adults. Minimum age is not stated on the homepage; check the Submit a Wish page. Wish fulfilled at no cost to the recipient.

  • With Eddie’s Help Inc. · Nationwide
    With Eddie’s Help Inc.

    An organization lead retained from the 2025 grant list. Amount unverified; a current individual grant program has not been confirmed.

  • Adaptive Impact · CA
    Adaptive Impact: Skydiving & paragliding

    A nonprofit helping people with mobility challenges, including ALS, experience adaptive paragliding and skydiving. Team Gleason shares an ALS participant’s experience with both activities. Free service.

  • ALS In Wonderland Foundation · AR
    ALS In Wonderland Foundation

    ALS community resources, support gatherings, and an equipment program. Free service.

  • Easterseals Massachusetts · MA
    Easterseals Massachusetts

    Assistive technology services, device loans, and financing programs. Repayable zero-interest mini loans: $100–$4,000.

  • ALS United Connecticut · CT
    Equipment Loan and Transportation Programs

    Free loaner durable medical equipment such as power wheelchairs and Hoyer lifts, plus short rides through Coordinated Transportation Solutions and van rentals for longer trips. Free equipment loans; transportation cost not stated.

  • ALS United Rhode Island · RI
    Equipment Loan Program

    No-cost loans of Hoyer lifts, standard/transport/power wheelchairs, electric hospital beds and air mattresses, rollators, AAC devices and Obi robotic self-feeding devices through vendors across Rhode Island. Free.

  • ALS United of Georgia · GA
    Equipment Loans & In-Home Respite

    The Ricky P. Sweat program lends mobility and communication equipment insurance won't cover. The Sally Panfel program provides short-term in-home custodial care. Free.

  • Joe Martin ALS Foundation · NC
    Free In-Home Care and Caregiver Coaching

    Free weekly home care visits by aides and nurses (as staffing and logistics allow), in-home caregiver training, ALS resource guidance, and Joe's Camps for kids and teens, for families in the Charlotte, NC area. Free.

  • MAC Angels Foundation · CT, NJ, NY
    Grants & practical services

    Practical services, transportation, advocacy, and community for people living with ALS. Amount not published.

  • The ALS Association · DC, MD, PA, SC
    Ramp Reutilization Loan Program

    Lends temporary, reusable steel ramps for home access, with financial and consulting help. Ramp loan.

  • ALS United Mid-Atlantic · PA, NJ, DE
    Scott A. Mackler, MD, PhD Assistive Technology Program

    Communication and assistive technology assessments, help with insurance paperwork for devices, loaner equipment for uninsured or hospice patients, virtual home and smart-home assessments, financial assistance for straight stair glides and modular ramping, and referrals to other funding. Free services; equipment loans free for uninsured/hospice patients.

  • The ALS Association · WI
    SMART Home Program (Wisconsin)

    Loans of Amazon Alexa-compatible smart home devices through the Equipment Lending Program so people with ALS can call contacts, control lights and outlets, stream media, manage lists and calendars, and control the TV by voice. Free (loaned); limited supply.

  • Susan Mast ALS Foundation · MI
    Susan Mast ALS Foundation

    Respite, equipment, meals, caregiver resources, and support groups. Free service.

  • ALS of Nevada · NV
    Team Curtis 4 a Cure Transportation Program

    Wheelchair-accessible transportation within Nevada for medical trips and non-medical outings (restaurants, museums, events, movies, family gatherings). Free (no cost listed).
