Grants and services just for California

  • Care for Kids Grant · ALS Network Rolling · apply anytime

    Small grants for tutoring, sports, lessons, school supplies and activities for children and young adults living with a loved one who has ALS.

  • L.L. Foundation for Youth Fund · ALS Network Rolling · apply anytime

    Respite care and medical equipment for the person with ALS, to lighten the load on young family caregivers.

  • Direct Assistance and Respite Grants · ALS Network Confirm with the organization

    Small grants for families living with ALS in California and Hawaii, arranged through your ALS Network Regional Care Manager: respite care (a short-term break for family caregivers), equipment needs, transportation, emerg…

  • Respite Care Grant · ALS United Orange County Opens in cycles

    $500 grants toward respite care so the caregiver of a person with ALS in Orange County, California can take a break. The money goes straight to the care provider (the provider bills ALS United Orange County), and a famil…

  • Adaptive Impact: Skydiving & paragliding · Adaptive Impact Confirm with the organization

    A nonprofit helping people with mobility challenges, including ALS, experience adaptive paragliding and skydiving. Team Gleason shares an ALS participant’s experience with both activities.
