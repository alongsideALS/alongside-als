Grants and services just for Florida

  • Patient Care Fund · ALS Recovery Fund Rolling · apply anytime

    Speech-generating devices (after a speech evaluation at the Kessenich Family MDA ALS Center), bathing and toileting equipment Medicare often won't cover, and respite care.

  • Care Assist, Daily Living & Transportation Grants · The ALS Association, Florida Closing soon · 26 days left

    Care Assist grants for respite, medical expenses, home and vehicle modifications and van purchases. Daily Living grants for assistive technology. Transportation grants for rides to an ALS Certified Center or affiliated c…

  • Respite Care for ALS Caregivers · The ALS Association, Florida Closed for now

    Pays a substitute caregiver (an agency or someone you choose who isn't family) so the full-time family caregiver can take a break.

  • Rick and Sherry Murray Medical Futures Scholarship · The ALS Association, Florida Closed for now

    For relatives of someone with ALS who are studying medicine, a health field or social work.
