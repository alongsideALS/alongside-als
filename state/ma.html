Grants and services just for Massachusetts

  • Equipment, Grants & Family Support · Compassionate Care ALS (CCALS) Confirm with the organization

    Equipment lending, family grants and support programs, based on Cape Cod with wider New England reach.

  • Foundation for Life Grant · The ALS Association, Massachusetts Rolling · apply anytime

    Paid directly to vendors for ALS costs insurance doesn't fully cover, such as wheelchairs, stair lifts, bathroom modifications, and speech or eye-gaze devices.

  • Flex Grants · The Susie Foundation Rolling · apply anytime

    Reimbursement for eligible ALS-related equipment, home modifications, care, and medical costs not covered by other assistance.

  • Family Grants · Jeff Coombs Fund Confirm with the organization

    Assistance for unexpected family expenses related to illness, injury, loss, or other hardship. Not limited to ALS.

  • Easterseals Massachusetts · Easterseals Massachusetts Available now

    Massachusetts Alternative Finance Program: low-interest loans for larger assistive-technology purchases, zero-interest mini loans of $100–$4,000, and free long-term loans of devices under $500 for people who qualify fina…

  • Free medically tailored home-delivered meals · Community Servings Available now

    Free scratch-made, medically tailored meals delivered to the home plus dietitian support for people living with a critical or chronic illness and food insecurity, across Massachusetts and Rhode Island.
