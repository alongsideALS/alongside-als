Grants and services just for Michigan

  • In-Home Care Assistance Program · ALS of Michigan Rolling · apply anytime

    Funds up to $500 a month of paid in-home care, either to give a family caregiver who lives with and cares for a person with ALS a break, or to provide care for a person with ALS who has no caregiver. Available as capacit…

  • Thomas E. Worford, Jr. Transportation Program · ALS of Michigan Rolling · apply anytime

    Reimburses up to $300 per calendar year of rides from independent transportation companies for people with ALS who can no longer drive or need wheelchair-accessible transport — for non-emergency medical appointments, fam…

  • Albert Burstein Wish Fund · ALS of Michigan Closing soon · 5 days left

    A one-time $2,500 grant to fulfill a wish that would make life more meaningful for a person living with ALS in Michigan (the fund honors Albert Burstein, who died of ALS in 1986).

  • Susan Mast ALS Foundation · Susan Mast ALS Foundation Confirm with the organization

    Respite, equipment, meals, caregiver resources, and support groups.
