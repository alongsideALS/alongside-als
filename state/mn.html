Grants and services just for Minnesota

  • Senator Tomassoni Caregiver Support Program · The ALS Association, Minnesota Closed for now

    Money toward in-home care, through a home care agency or a person you choose (not a spouse, parent or child), plus free ALS caregiver training.

  • Maurer-Heinsen Home Access Program · The ALS Association Rolling · apply anytime

    Pays a significant portion of the cost of a temporary modular ramp (vendor-installed) so people with ALS can get in and out of their homes. The family pays the agreed remainder; payment goes directly to the vendor. Ramps…
