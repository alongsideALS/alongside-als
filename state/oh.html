Grants and services just for Ohio

  • Quality of Life Reimbursement Grant · ALS United Ohio (Central & Southern Ohio / Northern Kentucky) Rolling · apply anytime

    Reimburses disease-related expenses such as equipment, care, services, respite care, counseling, healthcare co-pays and home modifications. Some items require a prescription.

  • Movement Disorder Patient Grant Program · West Virginia Movement Disorder Alliance Confirm with the organization

    Money toward the costs of getting care and staying mobile for people with a movement disorder, ALS included: travel to appointments and procedures, medical and medication copays, physical therapy, power chairs, scooters …
