Grants and services just for Rhode Island

  • Equipment, Grants & Family Support · Compassionate Care ALS (CCALS) Confirm with the organization

    Equipment lending, family grants and support programs, based on Cape Cod with wider New England reach.

  • Respite Program (annual stipend) · ALS United Rhode Island Confirm with the organization

    An annual stipend for respite care through partner home health providers, which may include homemakers, certified nursing assistants and house cleaning, so family caregivers can take a break.

  • Home Modification Program (one-time stipend) · ALS United Rhode Island Confirm with the organization

    A one-time stipend toward home modifications that improve accessibility and safety, such as ramps or bathroom changes. Partner vendors also offer special pricing on modification services and equipment.

  • Flex Grants · The Susie Foundation Rolling · apply anytime

    Reimbursement for eligible ALS-related equipment, home modifications, care, and medical costs not covered by other assistance.

  • Equipment Loan Program · ALS United Rhode Island Available now

    No-cost loans of Hoyer lifts, standard/transport/power wheelchairs, electric hospital beds and air mattresses, rollators, AAC devices and Obi robotic self-feeding devices through vendors across Rhode Island.
