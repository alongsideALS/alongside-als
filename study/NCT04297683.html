HEALEY ALS Platform Trial

Merit E. Cudkowicz, MD · Phase 2/3 · NCT04297683

HEALEY ALS Platform Trial - Master Protocol

What it studies
A shared trial framework that evaluates multiple potential ALS treatments under one master protocol. Individual treatment arms have separate registry records.
Who it is for
Adults with ALS meeting the master protocol’s timing, breathing, medication, and other criteria. The record specifies weakness onset within 24 months; individual arms may add requirements.
Taking part
Eligible participants are assigned by chance to an available treatment arm, then to active treatment or placebo. Procedures and duration depend on the arm. Ask the team which arms are currently accepting participants and which visits are required.
Treatments being tested
Zilucoplan, Verdiperstat, CNM-Au8, Pridopidine, SLS-005 Trehalose, ABBV-CLS-7262, DNL343, NUZ-001
