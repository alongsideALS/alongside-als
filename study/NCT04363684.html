ALLFTD (frontotemporal degeneration / C9orf72 families)

Mayo Clinic · Observational · NCT04363684

ARTFL LEFFTDS Longitudinal Frontotemporal Lobar Degeneration (ALLFTD)

In plain words
A North American consortium study of frontotemporal lobar degeneration, including ALS-FTD and asymptomatic members of families carrying C9orf72 and other FTD/ALS genes. Participants have periodic visits with cognitive testing, MRI and biosamples. Not a treatment study. Listed as open on UCSF and UCSD ALS trial pages (Oct 2026).
What it studies
An observational study collecting information about the listed research conditions. Researchers do not assign a treatment as part of this study design.
Treatment being tested
Longitudinal clinical, cognitive, imaging and biofluid assessments for people with FTD-spectrum disorders and members of families with FTD/ALS-causing genes
Key eligibility
  • Age 18+ with an FTD-spectrum diagnosis (including ALS with cognitive/behavioral features), or a member of a family with a known FTD/ALS gene mutation
  • See ClinicalTrials.gov listing for full criteria

Highlights only; the full criteria are on ClinicalTrials.gov.

Who it is for
Listed research conditions: Frontotemporal Lobar Degeneration (FTLD); Progressive Supranuclear Palsy (PSP); Corticobasal Degeneration (CBD) (and other listed conditions). Age range: 18 years and older. The record allows healthy volunteers, subject to its criteria. Other criteria apply; the study team determines eligibility.
Genetic ALS
C9orf72 and other FTD/ALS genes; at-risk family members
Taking part
The structured record does not establish the full visit, testing, travel, or time commitment. Ask the study team what information or samples they collect and whether any visits can be remote. The registry lists a target of about 2,100 participants.
Timeline
Started March 2020; main results expected around August 2030. Registry estimates; dates often move.
