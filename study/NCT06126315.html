ALCALS

Mario Negri Institute for Pharmacological Research · Phase 2/3 · NCT06126315

Trial on the Biological and Clinical Effects of Acetyl-L-carnitine in ALS

What it studies
An interventional study investigating whether an approach could help treat a health condition. Listed approaches include medication.
Who it is for
Listed research conditions: Amyotrophic Lateral Sclerosis. Age range: 18 years and older. Healthy volunteers are not listed as eligible. Other criteria apply; the study team determines eligibility.
Taking part
Assignment to a study group is by chance. The study includes a placebo comparison group. Visit frequency, travel, procedures, and time commitment are not fully summarized here; confirm them with the study team.
Treatments being tested
Acetyl-l-carnitine
Other countries
Australia, Italy

Registry record
