Augmented Reality BCI Longitudinal Study for Persons With Late Stage ALS

What it studies
An interventional study investigating whether a device can be used as intended. Listed approaches include a device.
Who it is for
Listed research conditions: ALS (Amyotrophic Lateral Sclerosis). Age range: 18 years and older. Healthy volunteers are not listed as eligible. Other criteria apply; the study team determines eligibility.
Taking part
Treatment assignment is not blinded. Visit frequency, travel, procedures, and time commitment are not fully summarized here; confirm them with the study team.
