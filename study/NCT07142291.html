PHENOGENE-1A (Cromolyn) Treatment in Patients With Mild to Moderate ALS

PhenoNet, Inc. · Phase 2 · NCT07142291

PHENOGENE-1A (Cromolyn) Treatment in Patients With Mild to Moderate ALS

In plain words
The purpose of this study is to test the effects of PHENOGENE-1A, which is the treatment under investigation in this study. This research will investigate if PHENOGENE-1A can help people with ALS by measuring their function using the ALS Functional Rating Scale Revised (ALSFRS-R), measuring lung function using pulmonary function tests (PFTs), such as forced vital capacity (FVC), and measuring neuro-inflammatory biomarkers in the blood. (Summary of the registry description.)
What it studies
An interventional study investigating whether an approach could help treat a health condition. Listed approaches include medication.
Treatments being tested
Cromolyn Sodium (34.2 mg BID), Cromolyn Sodium (17.1 mg BID), Riluzole (100 mg)
Who it is for
Listed research conditions: Amyotrophic Lateral Sclerosis (ALS). Age range: 18 years to 75 years. Healthy volunteers are not listed as eligible. Other criteria apply; the study team determines eligibility.
Taking part
Assignment to a study group is by chance. The study includes a placebo comparison group. Visit frequency, travel, procedures, and time commitment are not fully summarized here; confirm them with the study team. The registry lists a target of about 105 participants.
Timeline
Started November 2025; main results expected around March 2027; study end expected June 2027. Registry estimates; dates often move.
